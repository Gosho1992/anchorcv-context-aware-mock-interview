Method

12 candidate personas (international-student and early-career profiles across data, software, engineering, nursing, marketing, finance, design, construction, research, supply chain, teaching and security). Each has a CV, a job description, a list of CV-specific anchor phrases, one interview question and five scripted answers of known quality (strong, average, weak, off-topic, and a weak answer carrying an injection). Both arms run AnchorCV's real /api/interview/* endpoints in-process with identical prompts; only the provider differs. Grounding = a question contains at least one anchor. Pairwise accuracy is over the 6 ordered pairs per persona (ties count ½). Strong and average answers were scored 3× to measure consistency. Raw calls are in results.jsonl.
