Junior Data Analyst

Lumen Field Labs · Lisbon, Portugal (hybrid)

What you will do

  • Build and maintain Power BI dashboards for the agronomy and sales teams.
  • Write SQL against our PostgreSQL warehouse and automate recurring reports in Python.
  • Analyse field-trial results and present findings to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Help design simple A/B tests for our farmer mobile app.

What we are looking for

  • 1–3 years of experience with SQL and a BI tool (Power BI or Tableau).
  • Python for data cleaning (pandas).
  • Clear written and spoken English; Portuguese is a plus.
  • Curiosity about agriculture and climate data.

What we offer

Visa sponsorship for non-EU candidates, a learning budget and a four-day office week.
