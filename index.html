How is AnchorCV different from ChatGPT?
AnchorCV never invents experience. Every rewrite, score, and suggestion is anchored to evidence found in your actual CV, then passed through multi-stage quality gates (anti-detection, read-aloud, factual audits). You also choose the engine: GPT-4o or Claude.
Which CV templates are included?
World Bank CV, ADB Consultant CV (CMS format), Immigration/Visa CV for points-based systems, Lawyer/Legal CV, and Executive CV. Convert your CV into any of them, or run a compare-and-score to get a section-by-section gap report first.
Will my CV pass ATS screening?
ATS Shield simulates automated screening the way real systems parse your CV — flagging formatting traps, missing keywords, and parse failures, with concrete fixes before you apply.
What does it cost?
$9 per 3-month application season. Renew only when you need it — no auto-billing surprises. You can try the analyzer free before paying.
Is my CV data safe?
Your CV is processed securely, used only to generate your results, and never used to train models or shared with third parties. You can delete your files anytime.

Yes. The First Resume builder creates one from scratch: guided sections, drag-and-drop ordering, live preview, AI enhancement, and PDF/DOCX export.
